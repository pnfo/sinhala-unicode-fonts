Viriya (විරිය, Pali for energy or effort) is a narrow Sinhala display typeface by Pushpananda Ekanayake.

Its condensed letters fit long words into narrow spaces, for headings, posters, signs and titles.

The fonts are Unicode conversions of designs Pushpananda Ekanayake made for a legacy (pre-Unicode) encoding, released under the SIL Open Font License with his written agreement. Their glyph set and Sinhala OpenType shaping come from Abhaya Libre, so they render conjuncts, touching letters (bandi akuru), rakaransaya, yansaya and repaya, and the forms used in Pali texts such as ම්‍බ ණ්‍ඩ ඤ්‍ච. The Latin is adapted from the Noto project and includes the letters of Pali romanisation (ā ī ū ṃ ṅ ñ ṭ ḍ ṇ ḷ).

Pushpananda Ekanayake has designed Sinhala typefaces since the 1990s, and his fonts are widely used in Sri Lankan publishing.

To contribute, see github.com/pnfo/sinhala-unicode-fonts.
